Tatsujin Sans JP Regular is derived from the bundled Noto Sans JP variable font.
Original copyright and SIL Open Font License 1.1 are in NotoSansJP-OFL.txt.
The original font declares "Source" as a Reserved Font Name.

Modifications by PDF Tatsujin contributors:
- Instantiate the weight axis at 400 with fontTools 4.60.1.
- Rename family and style records to Tatsujin Sans JP / Regular and the
  PostScript name to TatsujinSansJP-Regular.
- Retain the complete Unicode repertoire, glyphs and original OFL notices.
- Disable timestamp recalculation for reproducible generation.

The derived font remains under SIL OFL 1.1, not the application's MIT license.
No Microsoft Windows fonts are included in this derivation or distribution.
Recipe: scripts/derive-form-font.py. Source and output hashes: dependency-lock.json.
